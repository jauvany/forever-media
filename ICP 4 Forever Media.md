Based on what I found, **Forever Media (forevermedia.io)** runs a performance-based short-form content distribution platform (powered by "ClipX") that connects brands/creators who want more reach with a network of "clippers" who repost content to TikTok/Reels/Shorts and get paid per verified view.

That's a two-sided marketplace, so there are really two possible ICPs depending on which side you're prospecting for. For lead gen purposes, the more valuable target is almost always the **demand side** — the paying customers who launch campaigns — since that's where revenue comes from. Here's a full profile for that side, with the supply-side (clippers) profile after it as a secondary option.

## Primary ICP: Brands, Artists & Creators Buying Clipping Campaigns

**Who they are**
- Musicians/artists, podcasters, streamers, coaches/course creators, and DTC or app-based consumer brands with existing video/audio content (podcast episodes, streams, long-form YouTube, live performances)
- Marketing or growth leads at talent management agencies, record labels, and creator management companies running campaigns on behalf of multiple artists
- Founders/CMOs at consumer brands (gaming, fitness, beauty, fintech apps, mobile games) using influencer-adjacent, performance-based marketing

**Firmographics**
- Small-to-mid size teams (1–50 people) — solo creators up to boutique agencies/labels
- Revenue or funding sufficient to fund pay-per-view campaigns (typically requires ongoing ad/marketing budget, not one-off)
- Active on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, X — ideally already posting long-form content that can be repurposed

**Signals / triggers to prospect on**
- Recently released an album, launched a podcast, gone live regularly (Twitch/YouTube), or raised funding/launched an app
- Already running influencer or UGC campaigns (shows budget + appetite for performance marketing)
- Hiring for "growth marketing," "social media manager," or "creator partnerships" roles
- Competitors or peers already using clipping/UGC distribution networks (Clipping.io, Whop clipping communities, etc.)

**Pain points Forever Media solves**
- Wants short-form reach without building an in-house clipping team
- Wants to pay only for verified performance (views), not flat influencer fees
- Needs real-time analytics/transparency on where content is landing

**Where to find them**
- Discord/Whop communities around clipping, creator economy, UGC marketing
- LinkedIn: titles like Growth Marketer, Artist Manager, Head of Content, Community Manager at labels/agencies
- Podcast/streaming directories for shows with growing but under-distributed audiences

## Secondary ICP: Clippers (Supply Side)

- 16–30 year-olds fluent in TikTok/Reels editing, often side-hustling or freelancing
- Already active in "get paid to clip" Discord/Whop communities, video editing freelance marketplaces, or CapCut/editing-tool communities
- Motivated by fast, transparent payouts (PayPal/crypto) and low barrier to entry
- Lead gen here is more about community/creator-acquisition channels (Discord ads, TikTok organic, referral programs) than traditional B2B prospecting
