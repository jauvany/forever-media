## 6. Things NOT to Do

- **Don't outreach without budget-qualifying first.** This is a $15k+ model — chasing small creators or unfunded accounts wastes cycles and skews your metrics. Confirm fit signals (funding, label backing, brand size, sponsorship history) before investing outreach time.
- **Don't send generic "we grow your views" messages.** At this price point, buyers expect specificity — reference their actual trigger event and a matched case study.
- **Don't rely on cold email alone.** Multi-channel (email + LinkedIn/social) outperforms single-channel at this deal size, since decision-makers are harder to reach and less likely to reply to email alone.
- **Don't skip decision-maker research.** Sending to a generic inbox or the wrong contact (e.g., an assistant instead of the actual marketing lead) kills response rates.
- **Don't over-promise specific view counts.** Cite real case study numbers as *proof of capability*, never as a guarantee for a new client.
- **Don't ghost after one no.** Budget cycles shift — tag "not now" leads for re-engagement in 60–90 days or around their next launch/release.
- **Don't neglect the press angle.** Forbes/Variety/Business Insider coverage is strong trust-building material — use it, especially with brand and label contacts who care about legitimacy.
- **Don't treat every vertical the same.** A message to a music label, a casino brand, and a podcast host should use different trigger events, different case studies, and different tone.
- **Don't let leads stall silently.** At this deal size, follow-up cadence over days/weeks matters more than daily blast volume — track and revisit deliberately.
- **Don't bypass qualification to hit activity numbers.** A smaller number of well-qualified, well-researched touches will outperform high-volume low-fit outreach in this model.
